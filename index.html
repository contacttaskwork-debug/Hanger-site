Features

A closet that finally knows itself

📸

Shoot garment after garment — AI fills in category, colour, warmth, formality and even the laundry pile while you keep shooting. Under 20 seconds per item.

FAQ

Questions

When can I download it?

Rail is in final preparation for the App Store and Google Play. The store badges light up the moment it's live.

Is there a free trial?

Every new account gets the AI stylist free for its first 7 days — outfits composed by AI, with a styling note, no card required. After that Rail keeps building outfits from your wardrobe for free, and the AI styling is what you upgrade for.

Do I have to photograph my whole closet at once?

No — start with what you wear most. Each photo takes seconds and you can add garments any time. Outfits get smarter as the wardrobe grows.

What happens to my photos?

They're stored privately — only your account can access them. Location metadata is removed before a photo ever leaves your phone, and images are analysed only to catalogue your garment, never to train AI models. Details in the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your wardrobe lives on your phone; changes queue up and sync when you're back online. Only outfit generation and photo analysis need a connection.

What if the AI gets a garment wrong?

Every field it fills is editable, forever. Low-confidence guesses are flagged so you know exactly which cards deserve a glance.

English only?

English and Spanish, from day one — the whole app, both legal documents included.
