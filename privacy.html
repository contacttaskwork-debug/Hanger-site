Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026

What Hanger collects Your account email and password (the password is stored only as a hash by our authentication provider). Photos you take of your garments and, if you enable precise washing, of their care tags. Your approximate location — a city name or coordinates — used only to fetch local weather. Your preferences: thermal profile, language, check-in time. Wear history for your own garments.

How it is used Everything Hanger collects exists to run the app for you: cataloguing your wardrobe, suggesting weather-appropriate outfits, tracking laundry. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose beyond the app's features.

Photo analysis When you photograph a garment or care tag, the image is sent securely to Anthropic's Claude API, which classifies it (category, colour, washing rules) and returns the result. Under Anthropic's API terms these inputs are not used to train their models. Location metadata is stripped from every photo before it leaves your phone.

Weather Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the local forecast. No account information accompanies them.

Where your data lives Your data is stored with Supabase, encrypted in transit and at rest. Your photos live in private storage only your account can access; database rows are protected so each user can only ever read their own.

Retention and deletion Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, photos, wardrobe and history — immediately and irreversibly.

Your rights You can read, correct, export or delete everything the app holds about you: your wardrobe is fully visible and editable in the app, and deletion is one button. For anything else, contact us.

Children Hanger is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you before the change applies.
